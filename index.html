1. Privacy

Your images

I do not operate a server for receiving, processing or storing your images. The extension does not send me or third parties your source images, outputs, file names, metadata or usage statistics. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, trackers or automatic error reporting.

Models, the runtime and fonts are included in the installation. Processing runs inside the browser and requires no internet connection. The extension reads only images you select or drop into the workspace. It does not request access to websites, browsing history, the clipboard or all files on your device.

What stays on your device

Only preferences are saved persistently: language, theme, palette, transparency, frosting, motion, reflections and the selected processing device. They are not synchronized across devices.

Selected images, outputs and history remain in the open tab’s memory. History contains file names, dimensions, processing times and the selected backend. The session is not restored after reloading or closing the tab. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

Removing an image from the queue releases its related temporary application resources. The browser and operating system manage memory, download records and backups. I do not promise secure erasure of every possible copy. Uninstalling removes extension preferences according to the browser’s rules and does not delete downloaded images.

External links and PayPal

The support button links to PayPal. The extension does not send it your images, file names or queue. If you choose to support the project, PayPal handles the payment under its own terms and may provide transaction information to me as the recipient.

The local copy of this page opens without contacting a website. When you follow an external link or install or update through a browser store, that provider handles connection data under its own policies. These actions do not upload your images through the extension.

The public copy of this page

If you open this page on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles connection information under its privacy statement. I have not added analytics, remote fonts or embedded third-party resources. This does not send the extension’s image queue.

What to expect from upscaling

Neural processing may alter or invent details, including faces, hair, text and fine patterns. A larger or sharper image does not establish that those details were present in the original. The output is not evidence of how an object actually looked.

Exports are 8-bit sRGB images. Original EXIF, GPS, ICC profiles and HDR are not retained. PNG and WebP support transparency; JPEG uses a white background. Animated images are unsupported. Processing does not guarantee anonymization: information visible in the picture may remain visible.

Processing uses device resources and may take considerable time, fail or produce unsuitable output. Available dimensions and speed depend on the file, settings, browser and free memory.
